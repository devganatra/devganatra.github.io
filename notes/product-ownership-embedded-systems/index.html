NOTE 01 · PRODUCT · 6 MIN

Product ownership for embedded systems.

Backlog ownership is useful. In mixed hardware/software systems, it is not sufficient.

DEV GANATRA
28 JUL 2026

Working note
6 minute read

The product is the behaviour of the whole system.

A customer does not experience separate backlogs. They experience one system.

Different clocks create hidden risk.

Hardware, software, and validation move at different speeds. The product plan must make those clocks visible.

Prioritise questions, not only features.

The next increment should answer a material question, not merely add output.

A practical operating loop

  1. Frame the outcome and boundaries.
  2. Map the system and decision dependencies.
  3. Choose the smallest coherent slice that produces evidence.
  4. Use the evidence to update direction and priorities.
