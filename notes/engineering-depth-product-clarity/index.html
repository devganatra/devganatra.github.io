Engineering depth, product clarity.

Technical expertise creates leverage when it improves the team’s decisions.

DEV GANATRA
28 JUL 2026

Working note
5 minute read

Depth should improve the question.

Engineering experience helps reveal the technical decision hidden inside a simple request.

Translate consequences, not implementation trivia.

Good translation preserves engineering truth while making consequences understandable.

Protect team ownership.

The product owner should improve the decision environment while engineers retain engineering ownership.
