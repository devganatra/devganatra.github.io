01Operating model

Make direction visible before asking teams to move.

A practical cycle for shaping complex technical work without separating product intent from system reality.

01 / FRAME

Clarify the outcome

Define the problem, desired behaviour, users, boundaries, and the evidence that would indicate progress.

02 / ALIGN

Expose decisions and dependencies

Connect stakeholder priorities with hardware, software, models, interfaces, integration, and validation.

03 / DELIVER

Plan for useful feedback

Shape increments around questions and integration evidence so the next decision becomes better informed.
