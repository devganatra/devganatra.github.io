01How I work

Keep the backlog connected to the product and its HW/SW system.

This is the cycle I use in sustaining work to connect stakeholder needs, system dependencies, implementation, and validation.

01 / CLARIFY

Clarify the sustaining need

Make the problem, affected users, product behaviour, constraints, and expected result explicit before prioritising the work.

02 / CONNECT

Map the HW/SW dependencies

Relate backlog choices to hardware, software, models, interfaces, integration timing, and validation needs.

03 / VALIDATE

Use system results to decide

Plan integrated results, tests, or measurements that reduce uncertainty and improve the next backlog or product decision.
