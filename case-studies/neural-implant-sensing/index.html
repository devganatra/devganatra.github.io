01 / QUESTION

Can existing implant wires provide additional sensing value?

The research investigated an electrochemical sensor method for neural implants.

02 / CONTRIBUTION

From experiment to interpretable evidence.

My contribution included experimental work and the engineering needed to turn measurement output into usable analysis.

EXPERIMENTElectrochemical measurements
ENGINEERINGData extraction workflow
ANALYSISMATLAB · plots · comparison
